# Script rules
1. Line one is a puzzle. No intro, no greeting.
2. BUILD: curiosity grows with true details (year, place, numbers).
3. YOU SEE: the real reason, starting with "You see" (or "দেখুন তো").
4. TWIST: one short surprising last line.
5. Short sentences, simple words, a twelve year old must understand.
6. Numbers as words. No empty lines. About 4 words per second, 35 to 45 seconds.
7. Every fact must come from the checked fact list with sources.

Example (the emu story, 1932):
HOOK: An army went to war with machine guns, and lost to a bunch of birds.
BUILD: In 1932, twenty thousand emus walked into the wheat farms of Western Australia.
YOU SEE: Emus run fast, scatter, and keep coming back.
TWIST: The army left. The emus stayed.
